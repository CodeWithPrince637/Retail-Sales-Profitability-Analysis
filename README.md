# Retail Sales & Profitability Analysis

## 1. Project Overview

I worked on a Retail Sales dataset to understand how a retail business is performing.

I analyzed sales, profit, customers, products, discounts, and shipping using Python, Pandas, Excel, SQL, and Power BI.

The main aim was to find where the business is performing well and where it needs improvement.

## 2. Problem Statement

The dataset contains 51,290 sales records from different customers, products, and regions.

The business needs answers to some important questions:

- Which product categories generate the highest sales and profit?
- Which products are causing losses?
- Are discounts affecting profit?
- Which customer segments and regions perform better?
- How do sales change over time?

Without proper analysis, it is difficult to identify these areas and make better business decisions.

## 3. Project Objectives

- Understand overall sales and profit performance.
- Find the best and worst performing products.
- Analyze customer and regional performance.
- Understand the impact of discounts and shipping costs.
- Identify business areas that need attention.
- Present important findings through an interactive dashboard.

## 4. Dataset

- Dataset: Global Superstore
- Total Records: 51,290
- Original Columns: 24
- Source: [Global Superstore Dataset – Kaggle](https://www.kaggle.com/datasets/apoorvaappz/global-super-store-dataset)

## 5. Tools Used

- **Python:** To work with and analyze the data.
- **Pandas:** To clean, transform, and summarize the data.
- **Excel:** To check data quality and analyze business performance.
- **SQL:** To validate data and answer business questions.
- **Power BI:** To create interactive dashboards.

## 6. Project Workflow – How I Solved the Problem

1. **Data Understanding:** Explored the dataset, checked columns, data types, and basic statistics.
2. **Data Cleaning:** Used Python and Pandas to check missing values, duplicate records, and data quality.
3. **Data Analysis:** Analyzed sales, profit, yearly and monthly trends, products, customers, and discounts.
4. **Excel Analysis:** Performed data quality checks and business analysis.
5. **SQL Analysis:** Used queries, aggregations, and joins to answer business questions.
6. **Dashboard Development:** Built three interactive Power BI pages to present the results.

## 7. Key Findings

- **Technology:** Generated the highest sales among the three main categories, approximately 4.74 million.
- **Furniture:** Had a lower profit margin than Technology and Office Supplies.
- **Tables:** Recorded a loss of approximately 64,083.
- **Orders:** Out of 25,035 orders, 6,125 were loss-making, around 24.47%.
- **Sales Trends:** Year-wise and month-wise analysis helped identify changes in sales and growth.

## 8. Business Recommendations

Based on the analysis, I recommend:

- Review discounts on products with low or negative profit.
- Investigate why Tables are generating losses.
- Focus on categories with better sales and profitability.
- Monitor loss-making orders and customer segments.
- Review shipping costs to find opportunities to improve profitability.
